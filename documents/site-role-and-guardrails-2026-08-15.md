# Sprinter Studio role and guardrails

**Decision date:** 2026-08-17

## One job

`sprinter.studio` is **Sprinter Studio — the venture studio of Sprinter**, working in the open. It incubates new products in two clearly separated tracks and publishes the record: hypotheses, prototypes, live properties, reusable patterns, and the evidence used to advance, revise, pause, archive, or stop them.

It is not the master Sprinter company site, a fifth commercial offer, a substitute for customer proof, or permission to keep every experiment alive.

## The two tracks

| Track | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Partner incubations** | New products Sprinter incubates with a named partner who brings the domain and the demand. |
| **Internal experiments** | Products Sprinter starts on its own bench, published while they are still unproven. |

No partner incubation is published today. The section ships with an explicit empty state rather than borrowing an internal experiment to fill it — and the site says so in the ledger, the FAQ, `llms.txt`, and the chat prompt. **When a partner incubation is added, the partner must be named.**

Any graduation sentence is scoped to internal experiments only, or omitted: nothing ratified says Sprinter carries a partner-built product into its own portfolio.

## The commercial routing rule

The Studio does not ask visitors to understand an operating-partner category, platform, diagnostic, workshop, or transformation mandate before Sprinter has earned trust.

The default route is:

1. **$2,500 Executive AI Accelerator** — one executive, two private 60-minute calls, and three repeatable workflows;
2. **$10,000 Portfolio Executive AI Accelerator** — five individually scheduled leaders plus an aggregate sponsor readout;
3. **$20,000 30-Day AI Operating Leverage Pilot** only when executive use reveals shared context, users, governance, data, or implementation needs;
4. **Sprinter Consulting** only when a named workflow is implementation-ready;
5. **AI Operating Partner or custom system** only when recurring demand and economics justify it.

The old AI Productivity Workshop and Portfolio AI Training Pack are superseded.

## Positioning

Supporting contract:

- A ledger entry may be an idea, prototype, tool, service, content property, infrastructure component, or active product.

## Public status standard

Every record should answer:

1. What is it?
2. Who is it intended for?
3. What is the current stage and status?
4. What monetization model is still a hypothesis?
5. What real signal exists today?
6. What remains unproven?
7. What evidence would advance, revise, pause, or stop it?
8. Does it support the current commercial funnel or distract from it?

Avoid “promising,” “strong fit,” “clear ROI,” and “launch-ready” unless the evidence is stated.

## Stage definitions

### Amble

A question or hypothesis under investigation. No build commitment.

### Sprint

A bounded implementation intended to answer one consequential question. Shipping is not the gate; evidence is.

### Sail

A live property with a reason to continue: repeated use, qualified demand, revenue, strategic reuse, or another explicit source of value. Sail does not imply meaningful revenue or a self-sustaining company.

### Archived

A recorded experiment, offer, or property that no longer deserves active attention. Its learning remains visible; it is not a current commercial path.

## PortCo Audit decision

The standalone PortCo Audit offer is retired.

The concept required a sponsor to understand and purchase a new diagnostic too early. Relevant demand now routes to:

- one $2,500 Executive AI Accelerator;
- the $10,000 five-leader portfolio pack;
- or Sprinter Consulting after a specific workflow has earned implementation.

The property may remain in the ledger as an archived decision but must not compete with the current offer.

## Operating priority

Studio is subordinate to the commercial business and validated product obligations. It should consume attention only when an experiment:

- supports Executive AI Accelerator sales, active client delivery, or a validated product;
- creates a reusable component or operating pattern;
- has unusually strong domain access and distribution;
- generates qualified demand or revenue;
- or answers a strategic question cheaply enough to justify the interruption.

Everything else stays in Amble, pauses, or stops.

- decision owner;

- next gate;
- support burden;
